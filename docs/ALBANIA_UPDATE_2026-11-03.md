# Albania 2026 — evidence update (3 November 2026)

**Cutoff:** 3 November 2026. **Case ID:** ALB-2026. **Scope:** Updates the [20 October assessment](ALBANIA_REVIEW_2026-10-20.md) of the ongoing Albanian movement. Other cases remain at their earlier cutoff.

## Coding decision

Keep `status=ongoing`, `campaign_style=uncertain`, `demand_scope=mixed`, `immediate_outcome=unresolved`. The evidence establishes continued mobilization and some violence at specific demonstrations but not the dominant methods across the full May–November movement. Police force is recorded separately from protester tactics. No final political settlement has been verified.

## Comparison with 2018–2019

The 2018 student movement retains its provisional `predominantly_nonviolent` / `policy_concessions` coding. Government policy announcements are not proof of fully implemented long-term reforms. By contrast, the 2026 movement combines education grievances with demands for the prime minister to leave office; its methods include campus marches and calls for boycotts as well as reported violence at some October protests. It remains `ongoing` / `unresolved`, not an observed victory or failure.

**Methodological limits:** These 12 cases were purposefully selected, not randomly sampled. No worldwide effectiveness rate or causal estimate follows from their distribution. The dated events listed above are illustrative, not a complete incident census. A valid campaign-style assessment requires systematic recording of peaceful and violent actions, organizational boundaries, and a second coder. Institutional action against alleged misconduct is not itself a finding that misconduct occurred.

**Next checks:** independently verify education-policy implementation; distinguish student-led and party-led events; monitor outcomes of official force investigations; document subsequent negotiated agreements (if any) and changes in demands with exact dates.
