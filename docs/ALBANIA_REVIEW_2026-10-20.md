# Albania classification and comparison review

**Reviewed:** 20 October 2026. **Scope:** ALB-2018 and ALB-2026 only; other pilot rows were not reverified. Latest reporting located: AP, 20 October. This is an evidence review, not an incident census or an independent investigation.

## Coding decision

Change ALB-2026 `campaign_style` from `mixed_or_armed` to `uncertain`. Retain `status=ongoing`, `immediate_outcome=unresolved`, and `demand_scope=mixed`.

The old label has support at the **incident level**: reporting identifies violent acts by demonstrators, independently of police conduct. It is not simply a mistaken inference from repression. However, the row covers a wider movement whose earlier phase was peaceful. The reviewed sources do not measure the relative prevalence of tactics across that whole campaign. Neither a confident mixed/armed label nor a confident predominantly nonviolent label meets the dominant-method rule at that scope. `uncertain` records this evidentiary limit; it does not deny reported violence or assert an armed insurgency.

An alternative, provisional `mixed_or_armed` coding could be defensible for a separately bounded October escalation wave. Do not silently substitute that shorter period for the existing campaign. Define boundaries, collect peaceful and violent events across locations and dates, and obtain a second coding review before deciding. No second-reader adjudication has been completed in this review.

Wire republications and reused Reuters/AP photographs are not additional independent confirmations. News selection can favor dramatic confrontations. No reviewed evidence establishes that police caused every violent incident, that all protesters were violent, or that provocateurs explain the violence. Claims about unidentified plainclothes officers require incident-specific corroboration and are not coded as findings.

### Historical source roles

- [Government, 26 December 2018: Pact for the University](https://www.kryeministria.al/en/newsroom/mbi-dokumentin-e-punes-mbi-paktin-per-universitetin/): primary evidence of announced decisions on tuition coverage, scholarships and university policy. Announcements are not proof of delivery or continued implementation.
- [Altin Raxhimi, Nature, 16 January 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-019-00144-7): accessible introduction confirms the education/science minister's replacement and that the controversial law was not repealed. Only the accessible introduction was used; the full article was paywalled.
- [Fitimtar Zekthi, 5 July 2025](https://www.richtmann.org/journal/index.php/jesr/article/download/14352/13963/48539): qualitative retrospective covering developments through 2024. Supports the distinction between concessions and limited durable institutional change; its causal interpretations are not adopted as established results of this pilot.
- [OBCT / Genta Madhi, 11 December 2018](https://www.balcanicaucaso.org/en/cp_article/students-protests-shake-apathy-in-albania/): contemporary account of fee grievances and class boycotts. The full page was accessible; it also reports abolition of the additional resit fee.

## What remains to verify

Build a dated event record with actor, location, tactic, source independence and certainty; include peaceful events as well as clashes. Resolve campaign boundaries before estimating dominant methods. For 2018, obtain implementation records for each demand, including budgets, tuition schedules and delivery evidence. Do not infer fulfillment from a press conference. Record disagreement instead of inventing counts or percentages.

The 12 selected cases cannot establish worldwide success rates, causal effects of protest tactics, or a predicted outcome for Albania. This review changes measurement uncertainty, not the movement's outcome.
